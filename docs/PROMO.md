## 4. 长版（官网 / 展览 / 媒体）

### 中文

ECHOFORM 是一个“声音建筑师”。它不播放音乐可视化，而是把声音当作一份建筑任务书。

一段 32 秒的声景，会被拆成 8 个乐句、5 次真正的停顿、一条基频曲线和一张频谱重心图。低频变成厚拱与基座，高频变成细柱与悬浮碎片，响度决定尺度，节奏决定旋梯的级数，停顿决定侧殿之间留多远的空。音色不是滤镜，而是材料：水晶、石墨、膜。

同一段声音，永远得到同一座建筑。指纹、几何、材质和粒子都由种子确定，因此它可以被分享、被比较、被重新走进。三种艺术方向——水晶圣殿、未来机械、流体有机——不是换色，而是三套完整的建筑语言。

你可以环绕它，走进它，让镜头替你穿行；也可以把海报、MP4、参数或一条链接带走。音频不上传，分析、生成、导出都在你的浏览器里完成。

**它不是一首歌的 MV。它是一座为这段声音建成的建筑。**

### English

ECHOFORM is a voice architect. It does not visualize music; it treats a voice as an architectural brief.

A 32-second étude becomes eight phrases, five real silences, a pitch curve and a spectral centroid map. Low frequency becomes thick arches and a heavy base. High frequency becomes slender columns and suspended shards. Loudness sets the scale. Rhythm sets the stair count. Pauses decide how far the side halls sit from one another. Timbre is not a filter; it is a material: crystal, graphite, membrane.

The same voice always yields the same building. Fingerprint, geometry, materials and particles are all derived from a seed, so the piece can be shared, compared and entered again. The three directions — Crystal Sanctum, Neo Machine, Fluid Organic — are complete architectural languages, not color swaps.

Orbit it. Walk through it. Let the guide camera carry you. Take away a poster, an MP4, a parameter file or a link. Audio never leaves the browser; analysis, generation and export all happen on your device.

**This is not a music video. It is a building made for one voice.**

## 6. Product Hunt

**Tagline:** Turn your voice into a building you can walk through.

**Description:**

> ECHOFORM is a voice architect. Speak, sing or upload a recording, and it reads pitch, loudness, spectrum, rhythm, pauses and timbre — then casts them into a navigable 3D building.
>
> • Low frequency → massive arches
> • High frequency → slender columns and shards
> • Rhythm → modules and stairs
> • Silence → empty halls
> • Timbre → crystal, metal or membrane
>
> Three art directions. Orbit, first-person roam and a guided camera. Export a 2560×1440 poster, an MP4 film, a parameter file or a reproducible link. Audio is processed locally and never uploaded.
>
> Built with React, Three.js and the Web Audio API. Every building is generated from a deterministic fingerprint: the same voice always yields the same architecture.
>
> We'd love to see what your voice builds.

Try the 32-second étude first — it has five real silences, so you can see the empty halls appear. Happy to answer anything about the mapping, the shaders or the export pipeline.

## 7. 展览 / 装置说明

**ECHOFORM · 声音建筑师**
互动生成建筑，2026
浏览器、麦克风、WebGL、实时音频分析

观众对着麦克风说话、哼唱或发出一个长音。系统读取声音的音高、响度、频谱、节奏与停顿，在屏幕上浇筑出一座可漫游的建筑。低音形成厚重的拱，高音形成细长的柱，停顿留下空的厅。三种材料方向——水晶、机械、有机——对应三种不同的建筑语言。

每一段声音都会得到一座确定的建筑；同一段声音重复输入，建筑完全相同。观众可以用触控板环绕、用键盘走入，也可以带走一张海报或一段影像。

时长：单次体验 2–5 分钟
设备：一台带麦克风的电脑 / 触控屏
空间：暗场，建议 2×3 米
